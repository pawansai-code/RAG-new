SC00003: Employee Travel & Expense Management System
Problem Statement

We need to manage employee travel and expense claims by:

Validating travel requests against company policies and budgets
Approving travel plans through multi-level managerial workflows
Tracking travel bookings, accommodation, and transportation details
Processing expense claims and verifying receipts
Calculating reimbursements, allowances, and applicable deductions
Generating accounting entries for approved expenses
Settling reimbursements through payroll or direct payments
Reconciling expenses with project budgets and cost centers
Tracking claim status, approvals, and audit history
Producing financial and compliance reports for management
