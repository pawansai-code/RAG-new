# SC00002: Bill Processing System

## Problem Statement

We need to process vendor bills by:
- Validating incoming bill data and matching against purchase orders
- Allocating costs to different cost centers and business units
- Calculating taxes (GST, TDS) on bill amounts
- Creating accounting journal entries
- Settling bills across multiple dimensions (by LOB, item, vendor, cost method)
- Reconciling bills and tracking their workflow status
